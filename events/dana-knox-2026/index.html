Research Poster

"Why Grandpa Doesn't Trust the Driverless Car: A Thematic Analysis of Older Adults' (Dis)trust in Technology"

Muhid Hassan Risvy (PhD Student, CS) · Alisha Pradhan, PhD (Assistant Professor, Informatics)
New Jersey Institute of Technology

We built a Trust Diagnostic Framework that combines thematic analysis of 86 real quotes from older adults (from 39 empirical studies) with explainable AI (Decision Tree + SHAP) to diagnose what drives trust or distrust in everyday technology — and what designers should fix.

"It's not their age. It's your design."
