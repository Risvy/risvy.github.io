Research

Latest paper

Muhid Hassan Risvy, Dimitri Theodoratos, Alisha Pradhan. Towards Designing for (Dis)Trust in Technologies for Aging. In Proceedings of the 28th International ACM SIGACCESS Conference on Computers and Accessibility (ASSETS 2026). Accepted. arXiv preprint.

Research interests

I study how people experience intelligent technologies. Right now that mostly means older adults: how they build, withhold, and repair trust in systems designed for aging, and what appropriate reliance on AI should look like for people who are not experts in it.

I work mainly with qualitative and mixed methods, and I try to turn what I learn into concrete design guidance for systems that are more transparent and more accessible. See Publications for the full list.

Earlier work

Before the Ph.D. my research was mostly in usable security and blockchain systems.

Security analysis of passkeys

I analyzed how passkeys are stored across popular password managers and found metadata leakage and weak encryption practices that can expose login data, visited URLs, and timestamps. I also built a browser extension that flags compromised or at-risk passkeys for each website a user signs in to.

Browser automation for improving security guides

I built a system that validates and updates public security guides, such as those maintained by the Electronic Frontier Foundation, when platforms change their interfaces or settings. It pairs open-source language models running on HPC clusters with Playwright-based browser automation.

Security analysis of software cryptocurrency wallets

Undergraduate thesis

My thesis examined security vulnerabilities and privacy practices in software cryptocurrency wallets, combining vulnerability assessment tools with manual and machine-learning analysis of user reviews. Supervised by Dr. Jabed Morshed Chowdhury and Dr. Sadek Ferdous Ripul.

COVID-19 vaccine supply chain

A private blockchain-based supply chain for end-to-end traceability of COVID-19 vaccines. The project reached the finals of the Blockchain Olympiad Bangladesh 2022; details and source code are on the Projects page.
