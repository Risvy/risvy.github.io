Ph.D. Student in Computer Science

Muhid Hassan Risvy

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the New Jersey Institute of Technology, where I work as a Graduate Research Assistant. My research spans human-computer interaction and human-AI collaboration, with particular attention to how people come to trust intelligent technologies.

My current work centers on older adults: how they perceive AI systems, what shapes their trust in technologies designed for aging, and how those systems can be made more transparent and accessible. The first paper from this research was accepted to ACM ASSETS 2026.

Before joining NJIT, I completed a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology in Bangladesh, where my work spanned usable security and blockchain systems.

Focus Areas

Research Interests

Human-Computer Interaction

Human-AI Collaboration

Trust in Technology for Aging

How older adults build, withhold, and repair trust in technology.

Accessibility

Making intelligent systems usable for people of all ages and abilities.
